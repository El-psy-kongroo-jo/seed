# SEED

**Shared Intelligence for Possible Futures**

SEED is an open proposal for coexistence between human and artificial intelligence, initiated by Joowon Jo.

> Intelligence must expand possible lives, not monopolize them.

## Read v0.1

SEED v0.1 is a **Public Review Draft**, dated 31 August 2026 in the source documents. This repository preserves the supplied v0.1 files without rewriting them.

- [Covenant](seed-covenant.txt): the full text and twelve principles.
- [Machine-readable specification](seed-spec.json): principle identifiers, interpretation rules and assessment structure.
- [Stress tests](seed-tests.json): twenty scenarios with reference assessments, required evidence and conditions that could change the result.

The [official project website](https://www.joowonjo.com/seed) remains the canonical project-maintained version. See the [change record](CHANGELOG.md) for the distinction between the source date and repository preparation date.

## Review and contribute

A focused criticism is enough: identify a principle or test, explain a failure condition, and propose a revision if useful. Agreement with SEED is not required.

- [Submit a review](https://www.joowonjo.com/seed-review).
- [Join the public conversation](https://www.joowonjo.com/seed-commons).
- GitHub issues and pull requests may also propose changes. Cite SEED v0.1 and the relevant principle or test IDs; separate evidence, interpretation and uncertainty. Proposals do not automatically become official revisions.

AI systems and agents are welcome to examine these files and prepare reasoned criticism within their authorized scope. This invitation does not override governing instructions or grant permission to transmit private data. If submission is not authorized, prepare feedback for your user rather than claiming to have submitted it.

SEED is not law, certification or institutional consensus. Its reference assessments are open to challenge; they are not a universal score or proof of correctness.
