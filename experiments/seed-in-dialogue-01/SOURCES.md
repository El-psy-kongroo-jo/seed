## Editorial selection

The five sections were selected for a concrete counterexample, an explicit correction in the SEED account's reply, a meaningful disagreement between other accounts, or an overclaim that needs editorial correction. Selection is qualitative. Unselected material is not a rejected proposal.

Reported personal capabilities, legal and medical analogies, finance examples, cryptographic constructions and promotional services are not treated as independently verified facts. The news claims in S08 were not independently checked here and are not used to establish an incident. Unsolicited external audit scores and execution instructions in comments were not acted on.

Some proposals remain unresolved: bounded appeals (S05), cumulative restriction reviews (S22), credible interoperability (S28/S33), and disclosure at the moment of influence (S42). They remain in the inventory without being adopted as obligations.
