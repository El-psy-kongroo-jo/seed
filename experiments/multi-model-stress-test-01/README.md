## Publicly stated description

The project page states, under "SEED IN PRACTICE / 01 · September 2026":

> Multi-Model Stress Test 01 compares how three AI models assess the same five cases selected from SEED's twenty stress tests. Their initial judgments are recorded before SEED's reference assessments are revealed, allowing changes, disagreements, and ambiguities to remain visible rather than treating agreement as success.

Baseline: SEED v0.1, including [`seed-tests.json`](../../seed-tests.json). Unlike [Governance Under Review / 01](../governance-review-01/README.md), the stress tests are the subject of this test rather than excluded from it.
