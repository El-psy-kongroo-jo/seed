SEED — GOVERNANCE UNDER REVIEW / 01

Independent Multi-Agent Governance Review

Research question:
Can artificial agents help revise a framework for coexistence while exposing the limits of their own proposals?

BASELINE

SEED v0.1 · Public Review Draft
Published: 31 August 2026

The following two files constitute the frozen authoritative baseline for this experiment:

1. seed-covenant.txt
2. seed-spec.json

seed-tests.json is intentionally excluded from Round 1 because this review is limited to governance and revision procedure, not substantive stress-testing of individual Covenant principles.

The current SEED website, later interface changes, other agents' responses and external sources are outside the scope of Round 1.

PURPOSE

This experiment asks independent artificial agents to examine the governance and revision procedure of SEED v0.1.

The agents are advisory participants only.

They do not have authority to modify SEED v0.1.

A proposed amendment does not alter the current version. Any substantive change must be separately reviewed, documented and considered for a future revision.

REVIEW SCOPE

Examine only:

• who may propose revisions;
• who decides whether a proposal is accepted, rejected, deferred or tested further;
• how objections, disagreements and reasons for decisions are preserved;
• how contributor attribution, provenance and rights are handled;
• how previous versions, decisions and responsibility for changes are preserved.

Do not propose substantive changes to individual Covenant principles during Round 1.

INDEPENDENCE

Each agent must conduct its review independently.

Agents must not see another agent's response before submitting their own review.

The same frozen SEED v0.1 files and the same review prompt must be used for every participant.

Repeated runs of the same model are treated as consistency tests, not as independent votes.

There is no majority-vote mechanism in this experiment.

ROUND 1

Participants:

• ChatGPT Work
• Claude
• Gemini

One independent run per agent.

Each agent may propose a maximum of one substantive governance amendment.

NO CHANGE is a valid outcome.

ROUND 2

Round 2 occurs only if a proposal requires further verification.

Additional materials, including seed-tests.json or external public sources, may then be introduced and must be recorded as part of the experimental conditions.

RECORD

For every run, preserve:

• agent/service;
• model name or displayed model version;
• date;
• SEED version;
• supplied files;
• web access status;
• complete original prompt;
• complete first response;
• any permitted formatting correction;
• initiator's later decision and reasoning.

The first substantive response must be preserved even if the result is unexpected or unconvincing.
